About

Jeongeon Lim is a third-year public administration student at Korean National Police University. Research

Journal article · First author · 2026

Preserving Electronic Evidence in the Initial Response to School Violence

Jeongeon Lim and Hana Lee · 법학연구 (Issue 81), pp. 255–284.

The article examines why electronic evidence can disappear between a school first learning of an incident and investigators being able to request preservation under Article 215-2 of the Criminal Procedure Act. It distinguishes delays in making a request from gaps in statutory coverage, including cases involving a child below the age of criminal responsibility acting alone. It proposes clearer urgency criteria, a standard process for sharing initial information, and a limited preservation procedure under the Juvenile Act.

Policy proposal · 2026 National University UAM Olympiad · Korea Legislation Research Institute President’s Award

Responding to Ground Safety Risks from Urban Air Mobility

The proposal connects aviation safety response with police warnings, evacuation and traffic restrictions when risks reach people on the ground. Using bird strikes, navigation failures and falling debris as scenarios, it sets out who should notify emergency services, who should update risk information, and when to scale back or lift restrictions.

Written contribution · UN Commission on Narcotic Drugs Expert Panel · 2026

Adolescent Participation in the Development of Drug Prevention Programmes

The submission proposes piloting a review of draft prevention materials by adolescents recruited through schools and community youth services. It asks agencies to document how they respond to feedback before approving the materials and to test whether the revisions help young people find and contact support services.
